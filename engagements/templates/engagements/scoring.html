Five-level scoring guide

Use customer-specific thresholds. Keep impact dimensions visible; do not average away severe consequences. Confidence is separate. Lack of recent news is not evidence of low likelihood.

LevelBusiness impactLikelihood for this customer
1Minimal disruption or loss; routine recovery.Exceptional exploit conditions; tightly constrained exposure.
2Limited loss or disruption manageable through normal operations.Difficult exploitation or substantial access/prerequisites required.
3Material loss, operational interruption, or customer harm.A credible exploit path with achievable prerequisites.
4Major interruption, financial loss, or lasting reputational harm.Practical exploitation, significant exposure, or applicable attacks occurring.
5Threatens business viability or causes severe, prolonged consequences.Applicable exploitation is widespread or attempts target the customer.
